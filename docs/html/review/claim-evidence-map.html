Claim and evidence reading map

This map accompanies Sparse interval certificates for parametric biobjective optimisation: linear cell bounds and quantitative continuity, by Antonio Clim, prepared for the Journal of Global Optimization. Affiliation: Department of Economic Informatics and Cybernetics, Bucharest University of Economic Studies, Bucharest, Romania. Correspondence: antonio.clim@csie.ase.ro.

The map organises the review material by scientific claim so that a reader can locate its statement, proof and evidence directly. It is a reading aid, not an exemption from reviewing any assertion or a certificate of correctness. The indispensable arguments remain in the article, its separate Appendices A-C and the thematic supplementary PDFs. Expanded technical chapters preserve the detailed records and procedures relocated from those supplements.

Here Main means Manuscript_JGO.pdf; Appendix A, B and C mean Appendix_A_JGO.pdf, Appendix_B_JGO.pdf and Appendix_C_JGO.pdf. ESM 1, ESM 2 and ESM 3 mean the three thematic PDFs ESM_1.pdf, ESM_2.pdf and ESM_3.pdf. Section and theorem identifiers follow those review documents. They are not new Online Resource numbers.

Using the map

For a mathematical assertion, follow the relevant Main and Appendix entries together with the listed supplementary proof dependencies. For an empirical or implementation assertion, read its design, acceptance contract and adverse outcomes before consulting the complete technical records. The expanded chapters are available for reading without running the software; reproduction remains a separate activity.

The purpose of the redistribution is to make the evidence easier to locate and to remove repeated operational detail from the scientific reading route. A smaller PDF page count does not by itself measure the difficulty of the mathematics or predict editorial screening or reviewer availability. No proof dependency in the table is designated optional.
