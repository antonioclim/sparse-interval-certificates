S14.7 R13 source and replay records

The historical R13 current_kit runs ten fixture-and-mode executions with the new geometric producer, including an isolated quadratic obstruction, lower-dimensional positive cases, stationary tangency, a 2,048-bit strict value and a forced universal fallback. Its default command is python3 run_demo.py --out results/demo. Producer and checker processes use isolated standard-library mode with no site packages. The final 112-file payload was copied by manifest to a fresh path containing spaces and replayed successfully on the same host: ten cases, 21 child commands and 11 separate checker processes, comprising ten acceptances and one deliberate digest rejection. Its source hashes were unchanged before and after execution. The exact command flags, input hashes, output proofs and failures are retained in the accompanying execution records.

The complete package additionally preserves the benchmark sources and frozen predecessor dependencies with their original relative layout. benchmark/EVALUATION_EN.md gives the common-task commands; benchmark/SELECTOR_REFERENCE_EN.md gives the separate minimum-query commands. independent/INDEPENDENT_AUDIT.md documents the separate-code oracles and counterexamples. The owner receipts and producer/owner_results/resource_contract_receipt.json distinguish correctness, malformed-object checks and process resource behaviour. Hash manifests identify the exact sources, but are integrity records rather than external authenticity signatures.

The development comparison producer_development_01 is retained as FAIL. One unequal-scaling proof exceeded Python’s default 4,300-digit integer/decimal conversion guard at the unchanged external checker. Its internal mathematical acceptance did not make it transportable under that limit. The revised exponent-doubling schedule avoids an unnecessary immediate jump to the conservative universal shift on those data. The final 36-fixture and 30-repeat batches passed with an explicit limit of 4,300 in both producer workers and checker processes. The separate universal batch used -X int_max_str_digits=0; all three cases passed. A controlled owner test also retains the final version’s universal-mode failure under the default limit and success under the unlimited setting. The limit may affect inherited intermediate event metadata, not merely final JSON.

The Seidel source was checked against the publisher metadata and Section 2 of the primary article. Its classical expected bound is not assigned to this Python variant without a separate analysis. The construction in main-article Theorem A1 and its supporting references and details in Online Resource 1, S12.2–S12.4, remain distinct from the executable. The general rational-point construction behind the local short alternative is also still unimplemented. No new physical data are claimed; Online Resource 3, S9.2, documents the retained adverse civilian pilot and its supplied source records.

S15.7 R14 portable replay and owner records

The portable kit contains a complete local runtime closure, ten recorded cases, a strict payload manifest and a runner which launches isolated Python processes. The default requires only the standard library. Its optional certified route can be run in the same isolated environment to exercise dependency-unavailable fallback; an explicit site-package option allows the installed numerical proposal dependencies. Every case has a separate checker process, and a deliberately altered digest must be rejected. The runner refuses an existing output destination. Instructions specify commands, expected statuses and safe handling of incomplete runs.

The component owners also reran their inherited banks: 290 geometry cases and 126 LP fixtures. Their checks are preserved separately and are not added to the separate-code bank’s counts. The separate-code audit prompted correction of omitted failed-proposal work counters and argument validation bypassed by an early successful hint. The producer now binds its baseline LP import explicitly to the preserved R13 file. These corrections preceded the final source freeze. No accepted false mathematical certificate was found in the executed banks.

The source records and final receipts distinguish portable replay, separate-code regression, same-host timings and mathematical proofs. None is an external-machine replication, formal verification or human peer review. Arbitrary-size exact decimal strings require the documented process-local Python setting; exceptions and resource exhaustion are incomplete executions, never negative RN answers. The optional proposal has no internal unconditional runtime bound. The all-row/all-vertex gate and exact clipping fallback retain quadratic geometric worst cases. Implementing the full deterministic near-linear backend remains a separate engineering task; the general rational-point constructor behind the local short alternative also remains unimplemented.

The targeted literature update separates the present global selected-support theorem from classical minimax and pointwise sparsity and from growing-dimensional matrix-parametric LP hardness. It is not an exhaustive priority certification. The adverse civilian pilot is retained without a new physical experiment.
