## Reading the scientific record

The main manuscript and article Appendices A-C carry the central construction, quantitative continuity and quadratic-obstruction arguments. Online Resource 1 retains the complementary mathematical arguments; Online Resource 2 retains algorithm semantics, measurement contracts and balanced comparative conclusions; Online Resource 3 retains extension results and bounded application evidence.

This documentation contains detailed records, formats and reproduction instructions moved from those thematic resources. Its section-level links preserve the original scientific identifiers. Reading the complete review evidence does not require executing the software. Full numerical records remain available even where a compact PDF now reports their scientific interpretation.

No new model inference, solver study, physical deployment, native Word/Windows test or external human replication is established by the document migration. A reconstructed certificate is distinct from a reproduced historical timing. An accepted finite certificate is distinct from a population or deployment guarantee.
